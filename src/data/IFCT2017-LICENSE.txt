IFCT 2017 COMPOSITION DATA PACKAGE ATTRIBUTION

The food composition records in ifct2017-foods.json are derived from
@ifct2017/compositions version 2.0.9, published by Subhajit Sahu:
https://www.npmjs.com/package/@ifct2017/compositions
Source repository: https://github.com/ifct2017/compositions
The package is licensed under the MIT License (copyright 2018-22 Subhajit Sahu).

The package states that its nutrient composition data was obtained from the
Indian Food Composition Tables 2017, and that food composition values were
measured by the National Institute of Nutrition, Hyderabad:
https://www.nin.res.in/

Only records with a positive reported energy value are included. Energy is
converted from kJ to kcal; energy and macronutrients are rounded for display.
The catalog contains 528 food records and is not a recipe database.
